# Balanced IsoSpring – Mechanism Design Project

This repository contains the final report and supporting documentation for the **Balanced IsoSpring**, a mechanism design project conducted during the Bachelor program in Mechanical Engineering (BA4 - MT) at EPFL.



---

## 🧠 Project Overview

The Balanced IsoSpring project aimed to design and dimension a highly stable, long-lasting oscillating mechanism to replace the traditional escapement in travel clocks. The final system features:

- A two-degree-of-freedom oscillator
- Force and moment balance
- Flexural pivots and compliant mechanisms
- Long endurance (11 days of autonomy)
- High mechanical stability and low wear for extended lifespan (>50 years)

This innovative mechanism eliminates energy losses associated with escapements by using flexural guides and mass-symmetry-based balancing. The oscillator is designed to operate reliably in various orientations and under motion.

---

## 🔍 Objectives

- Replace the low-efficiency escapement of a traditional clock with a flexure-based oscillator
- Ensure constant reduced mass and elastic return force
- Design the mechanism to be insensitive to gravity, acceleration, and orientation
- Achieve compactness suitable for a portable timekeeping device
- Meet a stringent set of mechanical and performance criteria

---

## 🧰 Key Design Highlights

- **Isotropic Elasticity**: Equal stiffness along both principal directions via careful geometric and material design.
- **Balanced Mass Distribution**: Ensures dynamic equilibrium under movement and rotation.
- **Dimensioning**:
  - Target frequency: 2 Hz
  - Reduced mass: 0.6 kg
  - Flexural pivots using Böhler K190 steel
  - Moving masses made from dense bronze alloy (Pfinodal)
- **Custom Gear Train**: Dimensioned to maintain power over more than 8 days (achieved: 11 days).
- **Minimal Wear Design**: Bearings and pivots are designed for long lifespan with minimal degradation.

---

## 🏗️ Construction Choices

- **Guides**: Crossed flexure pivots for large motion range
- **Material Choices**:
  - Flexures: Böhler K190 steel (high fatigue resistance)
  - Moving masses: Bronze Pfinodal (high density)
  - Non-functional elements: Aluminum Anticorodal 110-112
- **Precision Tolerances**: ISO:2768-f and H7/p6 fit for key assemblies
